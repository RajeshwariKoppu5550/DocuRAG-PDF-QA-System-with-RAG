XYZ Technologies Pvt Ltd - Company Policy Document

1. Company Overview
XYZ Technologies is committed to maintaining a professional and inclusive workplace.
Owner name is KOPPU RAJESHWARI
Effective Date: 01 January 2025 

2. Working Hours Policy
Standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
Employees must log a minimum of 8 hours daily.
Late login beyond 10:00 AM without approval will be considered a half-day leave.

3. Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to:
- Casual Leave: 12 days
- Sick Leave: 10 days
- Earned Leave: 15 days
Leave must be applied at least 2 days in advance.
Medical certificate required if sick leave exceeds 2 days.

4. Work From Home Policy
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
Must be available between 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

5. Code of Conduct
Employees must maintain professionalism.
Harassment or unethical behavior will lead to strict action.

6. IT Policy
Use only company systems for work.
Password sharing is strictly prohibited.

7. Salary Policy
Salary is credited on the last working day of each month.
Includes PF, insurance, and performance bonus.

8. Exit Policy
Notice period: 60 days.
Final settlement within 30 days after exit.
